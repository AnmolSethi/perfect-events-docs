Maps & Venue Locations

Put venues on the customer app's map, and choose Google Maps or the map tiles it draws.

The customer app shows events on a map (Discover → the map button) and a venue map with a Directions button on each event page. Both use the venue's map location.

Give a venue its location

On Admin → Venues (or the venue section of an organizer's event editor), fill in Map location. Paste either:

  • the coordinates — in Google Maps, right-click the place and click the numbers at the top of the menu to copy them (e.g. 51.5072, -0.1276), or
  • a Google Maps or OpenStreetMap link to the place.

Leave it blank to keep a venue off the map; its events still show everywhere else. Online events never appear on the map.

Map provider

Admin → Settings → App → Maps sets what the app's maps draw.

FieldWhat to enter
Map providerOpenStreetMap / tile provider (the default) or Google Maps. Switching takes effect on the app's next launch.
Tile URLA raster tile URL template with {z}, {x} and {y} placeholders, including your API key. Blank = OpenStreetMap's public tiles.
AttributionThe credit line your tile provider requires — required whenever you set a Tile URL. The map adds the © sign itself.
Use a tile provider for a live app OpenStreetMap's public tiles are fine while you test, but their usage policy doesn't allow heavy traffic from apps. Before launch, create a free or paid account with a provider such as MapTiler, Stadia Maps or Thunderforest, and paste their raster tile URL and attribution here. Changes reach the app on its next launch — no rebuild.

The app identifies itself to tile servers with its app ID (the APP_ID in your config file), as tile providers' policies ask.

Google Maps

Google Maps needs your own API key built into the customer app:

  1. In the Google Cloud console, enable Maps SDK for Android and Maps SDK for iOS, and create an API key. Restrict it to those two APIs and to your app's package name / bundle ID.
  2. Add it to the customer app's config file as "GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY": "your-key", then rebuild the app. Android and iOS both read it from there.
  3. Choose Google Maps under Admin → Settings → App → Maps.

An app built without a key keeps drawing the tile map, even with Google Maps selected, so switching the setting never leaves anyone with a blank map. Google Maps draws its own credit line; the Tile URL and Attribution fields only apply to the tile map.
