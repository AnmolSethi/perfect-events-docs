Documentation / Operations

Payouts

Manual payouts — the platform tracks what's owed; you pay organizers outside the app.

There's no automated money movement to organizers in this version. Payouts are a tracking and request workflow: the platform calculates what an organizer is owed, the organizer requests a payout against that balance, and an admin marks it paid once they've actually sent the money by whatever means (bank transfer, PayPal, etc.) outside the platform.

How the balance is calculated

For every paid order against an organizer's events, the order's payable amount already has the platform fee deducted (when the organizer is the fee payer). On top of that, a commission percentage is applied at payout time — either a per-organizer override (set on their profile) or the platform default (Admin → Settings → General). Balances are tracked per currency separately, since one organizer can run events priced in more than one currency.

Available balance = net earnings to date, minus payouts already requested or paid. A rejected request frees that amount back up.

The organizer side

Organizer → Payouts shows their current available balance per currency, with a "request payout" action — they choose an amount up to what's available (with an optional note) and it goes into a Pending queue.

The admin side

Admin → Payouts lists every pending request across all organizers. For each one:

  • Mark paid — with a reference (e.g. a bank transfer id), once you've actually sent the money.
  • Reject — with a note explaining why; the requested amount becomes available to request again.

Either action notifies the organizer.

This is a ledger, not a payment processor Nothing here initiates a bank transfer or PayPal payout automatically — "mark paid" is you confirming a payment you made yourself elsewhere. Building that connection to an actual payout processor (Stripe Connect, PayPal Payouts, etc.) is not part of this version.
