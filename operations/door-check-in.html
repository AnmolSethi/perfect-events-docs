Door Check-in & Offline Use

How door staff scan tickets with the Scanner app, what happens with no signal, and how buyers' tickets work offline.

Setting up door staff

An organizer creates scanner accounts under Organizer → Door staff and ticks the venues each person works. Staff can scan every one of that organizer's events at those venues, including events added later. "Revoke access" removes every venue and signs the person out of the Scanner app straight away.

Scanning

Staff sign in to the Scanner app, pick the event, and point the camera at the ticket's QR code. Each scan answers Admitted, Already used (with when) or a reason the ticket isn't valid. The event screen shows live door numbers — checked in vs. expected — refreshed every 20 seconds.

Find a guest

For a guest whose phone died or who can't find their ticket, tap Find a guest on the event screen and type at least two characters of their name, email or ticket code. Each result shows whether that ticket is already in, and a Check in button.

When the connection drops

A scan the server doesn't answer — no signal, or the server is briefly unavailable — is saved on the phone and shown as "Saved". The Scanner app keeps re-sending saved scans in the background until the server answers, so the queue at the door keeps moving. While a scan is saved, check that ticket by eye.

A chip on the event screen shows how many scans are waiting; tap it to see them or Send now. If a saved scan later comes back as anything other than admitted (for example, the same ticket was already used at another entrance), it moves to Need a look so staff can find that guest and check their ticket. Saved scans survive the app being closed.

Buyers' tickets offline

The customer app keeps a copy of the buyer's tickets on their phone each time the wallet loads. With no connection, the wallet shows that copy with a notice of when it was saved — the QR codes still scan at the door. The copy is deleted when the buyer signs out.
