Play Store Submission

Store listing checklist for the Google Play Console.

Submit the customer and scanner apps as separate listings — they're separate apps with separate audiences, not variants of one listing.

Before you start

  • A signed AAB — see Building Android.
  • App icon (512×512), feature graphic (1024×500), and at least 2 phone screenshots per app.
  • A short description (80 chars) and full description (4000 chars).
  • A privacy policy URL — required by Play regardless of what data you collect.
  • A completed Data Safety form — declares what data the app collects/shares (account info, device identifiers for push notifications, payment info if the app handles it directly for a gateway).
  • Content rating questionnaire, completed honestly for what the app actually does.

Submission flow

  1. Create the app in Play Console, fill in the store listing (description, screenshots, icon, category — Events, most likely).
  2. Complete Data Safety and Content Rating.
  3. Upload your AAB to a testing track (internal → closed/open, optional) or straight to production.
  4. Submit for review.

Review time

Typically a few hours to a few days for a first submission; updates are usually faster. See Common rejections for what most often causes a bounce.
