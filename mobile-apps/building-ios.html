Documentation / Mobile Apps

Building iOS (IPA)

A Mac is non-negotiable for this one — everything else is standard Flutter release process.

Apple requires Xcode to build, sign, and submit an iOS app — there is no way to produce an installable/submittable IPA from Windows or Linux. If you don't have a Mac, this is the one part of publishing you'll need to borrow, rent (a cloud Mac service), or outsource for.

1. Apple Developer Program

You need a paid Apple Developer account (individual or organization) before you can create the App ID, provisioning profiles, or submit to TestFlight/App Store.

2. Set up signing in Xcode

Open apps/<app>/ios/Runner.xcworkspace (not the .xcodeproj) in Xcode. Under Signing & Capabilities, select your team and confirm the bundle identifier matches what you set during rebranding. "Automatically manage signing" is the simplest path for most buyers.

3. Build

cd apps/customer   # or apps/scanner
fvm flutter build ipa --dart-define-from-file=config/prod.json

Output lands under build/ios/ipa/.

4. Upload

Use Xcode's Organizer (Window → Organizer, or drag the .ipa in via Transporter) to upload the build to App Store Connect, then attach it to a version in your app's listing there.

TestFlight first Distribute to internal TestFlight testers before submitting for App Store review — it catches device-specific and provisioning problems earlier, and doesn't count against your review submissions.
